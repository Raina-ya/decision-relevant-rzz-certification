# Average-metric insufficiency for decisions (candidate C7)

Status: **[theory, draft]**. Not owner-reviewed. Numbers are **[exact]**
outputs of `analysis/tolerance/average_metric.py` on the unlocked development
family; they are not hardware results.

## Statement

Let E(e) = exp(-i(eps ZZ + a ZI + b IZ)/2) be the diagonal residual of one
RZZ application, e = (eps, a, b). Its average gate fidelity is

    F_avg(e) = (|Tr E(e)|^2 / 4 + 1) / 5.

**Lemma C7.1 (invariance).** F_avg is invariant under the order-8 group G
generated by (eps, a, b) -> (s1 s2 eps, s1 a, s2 b), s1, s2 in {+1, -1}, and by
(eps, a, b) -> (-eps, -a, -b).

*Proof.* The eigenphases of E are phi(z1, z2) = eps z1 z2 + a z1 + b z2 over
z in {+1, -1}^2. The sign maps permute the eigenvalues (relabel z1 -> s1 z1,
z2 -> s2 z2), leaving Tr E unchanged. Negating all three parameters
conjugates every eigenvalue, so Tr E is conjugated and |Tr E| is unchanged.

**Proposition C7.2 (insufficiency, constructive).** There exist a
development decision instance with margin m and residuals e, g(e) in the same
G-orbit, hence with equal F_avg, such that gamma(e) >= m (harmless) and
gamma(g(e)) < 0 (harmful).

*Witnesses [exact].* With the same residual at both decision angles, on the
3-qubit path instance (m = 0.0773): e = (-0.25, 0, 0) gives gamma = 0.3526 and
g(e) = (+0.25, 0, 0) gives gamma = -0.0326, both with F_avg = 0.98756. The
4-qubit path instance has the same pair with gamma = 0.6850 and -0.0316.

**Negative finding [exact].** For the 2-qubit hardware-primary instance no
grid residual in the box |eps|, |a|, |b| <= 0.6 (step 0.05) has an orbit that
contains both a harmless and a harmful member. On that instance the sign
ambiguity alone does not flip the decision inside the box.

## Consequences for Ch4

1. A twirled or average-fidelity report, even if exact and specific to the
   angle, cannot by itself certify a QAOA decision: a decision certificate
   needs the sign of the ZZ residual, which average fidelity discards.
   Angle-averaged reports discard strictly more.
2. This supports G-A in `docs/literature/gap_map.md` with a construction
   rather than an assertion, as AGENTS.md requires for non-identifiability
   claims.
3. The 2-qubit negative finding is itself informative. The hardware-primary
   instance is less sensitive to the sign ambiguity than the extension
   instances. The OD1 choice should be revisited if the chapter wants a
   hardware instance where the average-metric argument bites.
