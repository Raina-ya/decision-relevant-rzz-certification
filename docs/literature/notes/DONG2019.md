# DONG2019 reading note

Reading status: relevant sections read. Reviewed the QAOA/Hamiltonian
uncertainty formulation, max-min objective, sequential convex programming
method, numerical examples, and conclusion.

- Question: Can QAOA controls be optimized to preserve worst-case fidelity
  when the form and range, but not the exact magnitude, of coherent
  Hamiltonian error are known?
- Model: the Hamiltonian equals a nominal term plus an error term indexed by a
  bounded uncertainty parameter. The uncertainty set is sampled numerically.
- Method: sequential convex programming repeatedly optimizes a local linear or
  concave-quadratic approximation to the worst-case fidelity over sampled
  uncertainty realizations.
- Evidence: single- and multi-qubit state-transfer/control simulations show
  improved worst-case fidelity and objective landscapes relative to nominal
  optimization.
- Limitations for Ch4: the examples are primarily robust quantum-control
  problems, not MaxCut candidate decisions. The uncertainty set is supplied,
  not inferred with finite-shot coverage, and the work does not certify a
  native RZZ implementation.
- Novelty implication: propagating a bounded coherent-error set through a QAOA
  objective and optimizing worst-case performance is prior art. The potential
  Ch4 gap is the experimental construction of a statistically valid
  uncertainty set and its prespecified decision classification.
