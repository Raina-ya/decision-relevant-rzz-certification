# RUDINGER2025 reading note

Reading status: full text read, including the experiment and appendices.

- Question: Can robust phase estimation efficiently estimate and calibrate the
  coherent parameters of a multi-qubit entangling gate?
- Model: a commuting two-qubit CZ model generated by ZI, IZ and ZZ, with global
  phase removed. The model is physically motivated but explicitly described as
  approximate; residual noncommuting terms remain after calibration.
- Design: three RPE experiments prepare superpositions of pairs of gate
  eigenstates, apply logarithmically spaced powers, and measure in-phase and
  quadrature observables. Three relative phases are linearly inverted to obtain
  the ZI, IZ and ZZ coefficients.
- Estimator: atan2 with branch selection from the preceding generation. An
  angular-history consistency check terminates analysis when a generation is
  incompatible with prior operational uncertainty ranges.
- Guarantee: under bounded additive error, the phase RMSE decreases with
  repeated-gate depth at Heisenberg scaling. The paper stresses that its
  consistency range is operational, not a statistical confidence interval.
- Hardware: pulse-level calibration of a superconducting CZ followed by GST.
  This is not an IBM cloud fractional-RZZ study and its optimization stage is
  outside Ch4 scope.
- Limitations for Ch4: the paper does not include idle-detuning controls,
  finite-shot simultaneous confidence regions, downstream decision tolerance,
  or a three-outcome fixed-budget rule. It post-selects leakage outcomes for
  the RPE observables.
- Novelty consequence: most of proposed C1 is prior art. Ch4 may cite or adapt
  the identifiability/linear inversion and confine any C1 claim to verified
  differences such as delay separation, model diagnostics, and projection onto
  a downstream decision. The Ch4 development implementation must not be
  presented as an independently novel RPE construction.
