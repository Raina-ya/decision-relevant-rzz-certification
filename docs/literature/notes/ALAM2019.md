# ALAM2019 reading note

Reading status: relevant sections read. Reviewed the noise and experimental
setup, MaxCut/QAOA construction, simulated depth/noise comparisons, IBMQX4
experiment, and conclusion.

- Question: How do realistic superconducting-qubit noise metrics limit QAOA
  performance as circuit depth increases?
- Model: reported gate error is represented by a depolarizing channel, with
  amplitude and phase damping derived from relaxation and dephasing times.
- Evidence: density-matrix simulations and small IBMQX4 executions examine
  cost/performance across QAOA depth and conclude that hardware noise creates
  an instance-dependent useful-depth limit.
- Limitations for Ch4: the gate-error model is aggregate depolarization, not a
  coherent RZZ angle residual; the hardware and toolchain are old; no
  uncertainty-qualified harmless/harmful decision is attempted.
- Novelty implication: QAOA task-level sensitivity to hardware-derived noise
  and nonmonotone benefit from greater depth are established background, but
  this paper does not overlap the native-RZZ measurement-to-certificate layer.
